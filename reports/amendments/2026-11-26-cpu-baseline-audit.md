# Retrospective scoring of frozen baseline answers

Libor “Burny” Burian · 26 November 2026

This analysis was specified after generation and before calculating either baseline's held-out accuracy. The starting revision is `fac5d9e788c33d066e1e92289fbf220e11e26625`. The two generation receipts, sample sizes, prompt coverage and timings were public. Synthetic fixtures had already identified permissive fraction and polynomial equivalence. The new comparison describes two prompts on a single frozen policy. Its release constitutes access to the primary holdout for future study design.

## Population and estimand

Use the complete ordinary and rising-sea prompted `exploratory-small-gpu-v2` archives. Verify the SHA-256 of generations, configuration and events against each [published receipt](../generation/README.md). Require exactly the registered IDs, input problems, families, splits, prompt identities and unchanged base configuration. Join by task ID. Report prompted minus ordinary verified accuracy, the paired four-cell table, format failures and counts by split and family. The unit is a problem. Keep the 200 fresh, 100 harder and 100 unseen-family problems at their original weights. Use 10,000 paired within-stratum resamples in sorted task-ID and split order with NumPy's default RNG seeded 20261115 and the percentile 95% interval. This interval describes variation over problems conditional on the policy, prompts and chosen strata.

## Frozen scoring rules

1. Use the original bounded verifier from the starting revision on each full response. Count incorrect and invalid-format responses in the denominator. Preserve the verdict and raw-record identity per answer. Record an infrastructure failure after the existing retry and withhold the affected complete-sample estimate until resolved. Never replace a failed generation with another response.
2. Freeze a secondary answer-form sensitivity check. A response must first pass the original verifier. For dice, require an integer or a literal numerator and positive denominator in lowest terms, allowing whitespace around the slash. For polynomial products, require a sum or difference of monomials in the requested variable, without an unexpanded sum below a product or power; harmless reordering, parentheses and equivalent coefficients are allowed. For real-root lists, require the complete set of values equal to the published four-decimal reference values, allowing equivalent decimal spellings and the empty list where appropriate. The original scorer tolerates a half-unit in the last decimal place around an already rounded reference. On other families the original verdict applies. Report each requirement's paired impact and per-arm count before interpreting it.
3. Independently validate the locked mathematical references and detect cross-split equivalences. Verify submitted expressions on constructed positive and negative controls. Publish item-level reasons and the strict-form sensitivity separately. No rubric assessment, theory score or transfer score is generated by this procedure.

## Reporting and evidence

Publish deterministic per-answer records and a summary under `results/scientific-audit/`. Preserve original generations, receipts, scores and dates. Correct a reporting or implementation error through a dated, attributable change. Any rule introduced after outcome access must be labeled post hoc. Report zero effects, reversals, unresolved records and exclusions explicitly. H1 and H2 retain their [original definitions](../preregistration.md).
