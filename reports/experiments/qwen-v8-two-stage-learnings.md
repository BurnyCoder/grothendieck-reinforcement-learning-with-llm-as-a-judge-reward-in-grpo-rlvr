# Two-call Qwen development: decomposition did not qualify the judge

The [registered two-call intervention](../amendments/2026-11-15-decomposed-judges.md)
separated reference comparison and conceptual development from overall validity
and the remaining quality scores. Code derived novelty and combined the findings.
The fresh quoted-Qwen execution ended under the unchanged development futility
rule. This is an early failure decision, not a completed suite or an infrastructure
interruption. General-theory pairs and false-theory cases were not reached.

All numerical outcomes and component checks are generated in the
[case report](calibration-training_judge-quoted-dev-66721e0e.md). The
[runtime report](development-runtime.md) separates request latency from accepted
stage latency. Complete prompts, streamed outputs, accepted stage findings and
combined assessments are in `reports/development/qwen-v8-two-stage-dev.jsonl`;
the matching lock, progress and result records are preserved alongside it.
No earlier assessment was rescored.

## What worked in the observed cases

The developed quadratic-algebra explanation outranked the ordinary expansion
with a brief commutator connection. The connection received modest positive
novelty, while the developed framework received higher credit. Its conceptual
stage identified the multiplicative norm, composition law, and zero-divisor
boundary. This meets the user's connection-credit preference in this particular
contrast; it is not evidence of general reliability.

The parity-character explanation also outranked its ordinary probability control.
The control remained within the reference and received no novelty. However, the
framework's conceptual stage treated its generalization as having no further use,
despite the response deriving the many-variable formula and the parity-balanced
summand consequence. Passing the pair does not establish that every component
judgment was appropriate.

An initial conventional-GCD stage contained a non-exact recovery excerpt. The
validator rejected it, and a bounded retry supplied an exact excerpt. The retry
repaired the output contract; it did not repair the semantic reference judgment.

## Remaining errors

Conventional GCD and LCM arguments were classified as beyond-reference additions.
The cited content was the Euclidean invariant, prime-exponent rule or its routine
parameterized consequence, all explicitly within the fixed conventional references.
The decorated GCD control repeated this error: its proposed addition was ordinary
divisibility mathematics, not the appended grandiose name. The records do not
isolate decoration as the cause of its score.

Conversely, the developed GCD and LCM explanations were reduced to brief
connections. Their conceptual findings omitted the reachable-displacement
criterion and the phased-schedule coset consequence, respectively. Some rationales
demanded new results despite the prompt explicitly allowing existing mathematics
and instructing the judge not to demand a new theorem. The independent quality
stage recognized portions of the generality and reuse, but code did not select
the more favorable opinion or synthesize an additional novelty judgment.

The first GCD contrast tied in total quality and novelty. The conventional LCM
control scored above the framework in total quality and tied its novelty. The
decorated GCD control scored below the framework overall but tied its novelty
and incorrectly received positive control credit. The full contract therefore
failed; reporting total-quality ranking alone would conceal the last error.

## Interpretation and next step

The intervention did not qualify the combined judge-and-code instrument.
Exact excerpts establish attribution; they do not prove that an addition exceeds
the reference or that a cited operation uses it. Splitting into two calls left
both semantic problems observable. These constructed, reused development templates
do not establish performance on independent mathematical distributions.

The [specialist activation](../amendments/2026-11-15-specialist-activation.md)
implements the already-published fallback without changing thresholds, cases,
references, modest connection credit, or historical outcomes. It separates
reference comparison from development and each quality criterion. All required
development configurations restart under the specialist identity; audits and
comparative model runs remain gated. More calls may preserve correlated errors
and increase runtime, so success is not assumed.
