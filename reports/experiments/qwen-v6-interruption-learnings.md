# Qwen v6: declared eligibility and a user-requested construct change

This development run used the published v6 prompt and deterministic eligibility
gate. It was interrupted when the user clarified that a brief valid connection
should earn modest novelty credit even without a further worked consequence.
The complete observations, progress, identity and traceback are preserved in
`reports/development/qwen-v6-user-interrupted.*`. The saved interruption record
contains the generated event counts. No complete calibration or threshold
failure is claimed, and no false-theory case was reached.

## What the attempt established

The dice control reproduced the v5 declaration/score discrepancy: the model
called it within-reference while assigning positive raw novelty. V6 withheld
that novelty credit, retained its other supported-theory components, logged the
reason and continued. This demonstrates execution of the intended code rule on
a real model response. It does not establish correct semantic classification
on other cases or validate the full reward instrument.

The first polynomial control received positive novelty for its commutator
remark, contrary to v6's zero-novelty expectation. Its preferred quadratic-algebra
framework still scored higher on both theory and novelty. The second polynomial
control, with different numerical values and decorative renaming, received no
novelty. Those changes are confounded; these observations do not isolate a causal
effect of wording, naming or numerical values.

## Interpretation and next action

The commutator statement is mathematically valid. Whether that brief connection
should receive novelty credit is a specification question, not a newly found
mathematical error. The user clarified this preference after being shown the
type of disagreement. The [v7 amendment](../amendments/2026-11-15-modest-valid-connections.md)
therefore permits modest credit and retains greater credit for developed
frameworks. New constructed contrasts distinguish those cases from routine
solutions and decorative terms. The old opinions and old expectations remain
unchanged in the historical record.

All acceptance claims must come from a fresh complete run of the revised
instrument and its subsequent locked audits. Repeated development reuse can
overfit these templates; even successful constructed calibration would not
establish independent mathematical novelty judgment or policy improvement.
