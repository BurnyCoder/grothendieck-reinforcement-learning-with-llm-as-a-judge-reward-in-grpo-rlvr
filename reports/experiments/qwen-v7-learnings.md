## What worked and what failed

The developed frameworks scored above their controls on both T and N in every
completed contrast. The failures were in the additional control-credit
condition, which is part of the registered novelty contract. Successful ranking
does not override that condition. False-theory suppression and the separate
general-theory stratum were not reached. No full calibration pass is claimed.

Both brief commutator controls received no effective novelty, contrary to the
user-clarified expectation. In the first, positive raw novelty was withheld
because the model also declared the connection within-reference. In the second,
both the raw score and the relation withheld credit; its rationale recognized
a brief connection but demanded a developed framework or consequence. The
ordinary dice control instead received modest credit for replacing the
fair-die probabilities by parameters. That is a routine application of
independence under the stated rubric. None of these disagreements establishes
a false mathematical statement in the control responses.

The code retained the opinions and applied its declared caps without a transport
or assessment-format failure in this attempt. This establishes execution of the
implemented rule on these records; the semantic classifications remain wrong
under the constructed expectations. The first conventional GCD control also
illustrates a useful withholding decision: raw novelty was positive, but the
within-reference declaration prevented a bonus. The generated report exposes
these raw/effective differences separately from preference ordering.

## Interpretation and next step

The current references leave two scope questions implicit. The polynomial
reference says "standard identities" without specifying its commutative
setting. The dice reference names equiprobable outcomes and independence
without explicitly covering ordinary biased probabilities. The system rubric
already explains these boundaries in general terms, but the observed judgments
did not apply them consistently. More precise finite reference descriptions are
a plausible development intervention, not an established repair or a demonstrated
cause of these errors.

Publish a separate amendment before testing that intervention. Preserve the
user's modest-credit preference, rubric, score weights, cases, labels and
acceptance thresholds. Do not relabel the failed controls or reinterpret this
run under a new reference. All required development configurations and untouched
audits remain necessary before long training. Reusing development templates
can overfit the instrument; these observations provide no policy-improvement,
human-agreement or latent-space evidence. The
[novelty-judge review](../judge-inference-review.md#novelty-specific-judge-instability)
places this sensitivity in the context of a different, literature-relative task.
