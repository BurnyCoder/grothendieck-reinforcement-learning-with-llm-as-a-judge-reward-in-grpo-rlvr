# Qwen v4 with bounded reasoning: failed novelty discrimination

Date: 2026-11-15. Development evidence only; no locked audit or comparative
policy evaluation has begun.

The [registered bounded-reasoning intervention](../amendments/2026-11-15-bounded-judge-reasoning.md)
reached the unchanged development futility condition. Counts, component scores
and the exact remaining-case bound are generated in the
[case report](calibration-training_judge-quoted-dev-08145e59.md).
The [full streamed event log](../development/qwen-reasoning512-v4-dev.jsonl)
and [result](../development/calibration-qwen-reasoning512-v4-dev.json) preserve
the failed attempt. General-theory contrasts and deliberately false-theory cases
were not reached; their performance remains unmeasured.

The GCD judgment rejected the lattice of integer linear combinations as a
departure because it was standard Bézout theory. That extends the supplied
reference beyond its listed methods and routine variations. The construction
also characterizes which displacements are reachable and distinguishes signed
from nonnegative moves. Conversely, the conventional control received novelty
credit despite reproducing the explicitly listed Euclidean invariant. Its
rationale denied a departure but still called the argument a modest reframing.
This is evidence of inconsistent application of the operational reference,
not evidence for or against historical originality.

The subgroup/coset LCM contrast passed. In the polynomial contrast, the judge
preferred the quadratic-algebra construction on total theory quality but also
credited the conventional control's noncommutative-boundary remark as novel.
That remark is mathematically valid and its classification against the short
reference remains debatable, as in the prior Gemma report. Labels and scores
are retained unchanged. Unlike the interrupted non-thinking attempt, the judge
recognized the conventional polynomial explanation as supported theory. This
limited improvement did not validate the complete reward instrument.

The client recorded separate thought and visible-answer channels. An invalid
supporting excerpt triggered the existing validation retry, after which a valid
assessment was accepted. The schema/evidence checks did not prevent semantic
grading errors. Native reasoning at this registered budget did not repair the
development calibration. Different partial attempts are not a paired statistical
comparison, and this finding does not establish that thinking generally worsens
judgment or that a larger budget would help.

Next, investigate an explicit reference-comparison decision before numerical
novelty scoring, while keeping validity independent of novelty. The
[inference review](../judge-inference-review.md) explains why executable
consistency checks may help and what they cannot prove. Any implementation must
be registered and published before fresh development inference. Do not run the
remaining model variants under this failed protocol, alter failed labels, or
proceed to long training. Repeated refinement against these public templates
risks overfitting; even a future audit pass would establish only narrow contract
compliance, not broad mathematical reliability.
